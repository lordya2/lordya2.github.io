On November 6, 2026, Huck Lee visited Florida State University’s business school in Tallahassee to present joint research with Eric Park and Timothy H. Rainer.

The team analyzed more than 12 million visits to Hong Kong’s public emergency departments before and after the city raised the fee from HK$100 to HK$180 in 2017. They estimated that walk-in visits fell by about 11% and found no statistically significant change in arrivals by ambulance.
